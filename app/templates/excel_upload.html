Excel Formula Support Web Compatible

Your Excel formulas are automatically converted to web-based calculations. Here are some examples:

VLOOKUP Product Information

=VLOOKUP(D2,ProductMaster!A:G,2,FALSE)

Automatically looks up product names, prices, and specifications from your product master data.

Shortage/Surplus Calculation

=H2-G2

Automatically calculates shortage or surplus by comparing delivered quantity to PO quantity.

Status Determination

=IF(I2>=0,"Complete","Shortage")

Automatically sets order status based on delivery completion.

Total Value Calculation

=G2*K2

Calculates total order value by multiplying quantity and unit price.
