# Product Overview

## KOL Influencer Management System

A comprehensive platform for managing Key Opinion Leader (KOL) campaigns from initial contact through final reporting.

### Core Capabilities

-   **User Role Management**: Role-based access control with different permission levels (Admin, Campaign Manager, Account Executive, Viewer) for team collaboration and workflow management
-   **Campaign Management**: Create campaigns from client briefs with budget allocation, timeline planning, KPI targets, and deliverable tracking before KOL assignment
-   **KOL Database Management**: Search, filter, and manage influencer profiles with multiple data sources:
    -   Manual entry and profile creation
    -   Bulk import from CSV/Excel files with data validation
    -   API-based KOL discovery and import from social media platforms
    -   Third-party influencer database API integration
    -   Automatic profile enrichment from social media APIs
    -   Duplicate detection and merge functionality
    -   Data quality validation and cleanup tools
-   **AI-Powered KOL Recommendation**: Automated KOL selection based on client brief analysis, campaign objectives, historical performance data, engagement rates, audience demographics, and past campaign success rates
-   **Performance Tracking**: Automated social media scraping across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and X/Twitter with configurable scheduling:
    -   One-click manual refresh for immediate updates
    -   Scheduled daily automatic scraping at specified time
    -   Custom interval scheduling (hourly, daily, weekly)
    -   Historical data tracking and trend analysis
-   **KOL Brief Management**: Template-based brief creation for individual KOLs, versioning, and approval workflows based on campaign requirements
-   **Multi-Channel Communication**: Automated distribution via Email, Line, Discord, and social media DMs with preference tracking
-   **Follow-up Automation**: Scheduled reminders at 1, 3, and 5-day intervals with communication history
-   **Calendar & Timeline Management**: Project visualization, KOL availability scheduling, and deadline tracking
-   **Content Monitoring**: Post detection, automated stat collection at multiple intervals (24hr, 3-day, 5-day, 7-day)
-   **Analytics & Reporting**: Campaign ROI calculation, KPI tracking, and automated report generation with customizable templates:
    -   Export reports as PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF format
    -   Custom report templates per client/campaign
    -   Template builder for Account Executives to design layouts
    -   AI-powered template generation from sample files
    -   Upload example reports to auto-generate matching templates
    -   Template library with reusable designs
    -   Dynamic data population (charts, tables, KPIs, images)

### Campaign Workflow

1. **Client Brief Intake**: Receive and document client campaign requirements, objectives, and budget
2. **Campaign Creation**: Set up campaign structure with timeline, KPIs, deliverables, and budget allocation
3. **KOL Selection**: Manual search/filter or AI-powered recommendation system that analyzes client brief and suggests optimal KOLs based on:
    - Campaign objectives and target audience
    - KOL niche alignment and audience demographics
    - Historical performance metrics (engagement rate, conversion rate, ROI)
    - Past campaign success rates and reliability
    - Budget fit and availability
    - Content quality and brand safety scores
4. **KOL Brief Creation**: Generate customized briefs for selected KOLs based on campaign parameters
5. **Communication & Follow-up**: Distribute briefs and manage KOL responses
6. **Content Monitoring**: Track posted content and collect performance metrics
7. **Reporting**: Generate campaign reports with ROI analysis and KPI achievement
    - Select or create custom report template
    - Export as PowerPoint or PDF
    - Automatically populate with campaign data, charts, and metrics
    - Save templates for future campaigns

### User Roles

-   **Admin**: Full system access, user management, system configuration
-   **Campaign Manager**: Create/manage campaigns, assign KOLs, approve briefs and content
-   **Account Executive**: Manage KOL communication, create briefs, track follow-ups
-   **Viewer**: Read-only access to campaigns and reports

### Target Users

Marketing teams and agencies managing influencer campaigns at scale, requiring systematic tracking and communication across hundreds of KOLs simultaneously.
